Festival ad calendar 2027: when to start your ads

People start searching for festive products weeks before the day. If your ads start on the day itself, you've missed most of the season. Here are the 2027 dates and when to start.

By the AdSure ad team · Updated 24 November 2026 · 4 min read

2027 dates and when to start

"Start ads by" is three weeks before each date. That's a good starting point for most products. Gifts and big purchases often need longer, and some products peak much earlier.

*Eid depends on the moon and can move by a day. Some festivals, like Raksha Bandhan, Janmashtami, Ganesh Chaturthi and Navratri, fall a day apart in different regional calendars. Republic Day is a date when many online sales run, not a festival. Check your local calendar before you plan big spends.

How to plan around a festival

  1. Start early, with a small budget. Launch two to three weeks before. This gives Meta and Google time to learn who buys, before costs go up.
  2. Raise the budget when searches rise. Interest usually climbs steadily in the last 1 to 2 weeks. Spend more when people are actively looking, not before.
  3. Expect higher costs at peak. In the days before Diwali many brands compete for the same people, so each click or sale can cost more. Have your best creative and offer ready before then.
  4. Stop or lower spend after the day. Interest drops quickly once a festival has passed. Check delivery dates: an order that can't arrive in time is a refund waiting to happen.
  5. Plan creatives in advance. Make festive versions of your best ads early, and check them before the rush. Ads can take time to get approved.
Your product's own timing. Every product peaks differently.

Lime bars need fixing first.

ProblemThe kurta only shows up after 4 seconds of the video

FixShow the product in the first second. Most people scroll past before then.

ProblemThe ad said “100% guaranteed”

FixUse real customer reviews instead. Claims like this break Indian ad rules (ASCI) and Meta's policy.

ProblemThe ad says ₹899, but the page shows ₹1,199

FixMatch the price and offer on the page to the ad, or people leave without buying.

Questions

Simple answers

Can't find yours? Write to hi@adsure.in.

Do I need to know marketing to use AdSure?

No. Share your ad and we explain every problem in simple words, with the fix.

Do I have to fill in my whole campaign again?

No. Start with your image or video, your ad text and your landing page link. It takes about 2 minutes. Add targeting and budget details only if you want a deeper review.

Who reviews my ad?

Our ad team, with 15+ years of running ads, and our AI. The AI is built on Meta and Google rules, real brand cases, India benchmarks and expert playbooks. Our experts check what rules can't judge, like whether your creative will actually sell.

Who runs AdSure?

AdSure is an Indian company, made in India, run by a team of ad experts who have grown businesses from ₹1 Cr to ₹100 Cr. You can reach us anytime at hi@adsure.in.

What else does AdSure do, besides reviewing my ad?

AdSure also finds your competitors and shows the ads they're running right now on Meta and Google, works out the best time of year to run your ads from 5 years of Google searches in India, and checks your website or landing page on a phone for speed, trust and a match with your ad. All of it is included in the plan.

Can AdSure get my ad account banned?

No. AdSure never logs in to your Meta or Google ad account, never asks for your password and never changes anything for you. You share your ad with us, we tell you what to fix, and you make the change yourself. So nothing AdSure does can put your ad account at risk.

Does AdSure run my ads for me?

No, and that's on purpose. Tools that run your ads need full access to your ad account. AdSure keeps your account only in your hands: our experts and AI show you exactly what to fix, and you stay in control. If you want a person to talk it through, you can book an expert call.

Is AdSure worth it if my ad budget is small?

Yes. Small budgets are where mistakes hurt most. On ₹15,000 a month, one ad that doesn't work can burn about ₹3,500 in a week. AdSure costs ₹53 a day and checks every ad before you spend, and the plan includes 5 full expert reviews a month, for about the price of one 30-minute expert call.

I'm not running ads yet. Is AdSure for me?

Yes. Before your first ads is the cheapest time to get them right. Get your first ad reviewed, see what competitors are running and find the best time to start, so your first rupee goes behind an ad that's ready.

Will AdSure get me more sales?

No one can honestly promise sales. AdSure helps you fix weak creatives, unclear ad text, slow landing pages and setup mistakes before you spend, and shows you what competitors are running and the best time to spend.

How is this different from Meta or Google's suggestions?

Their suggestions mostly tune settings inside their own platform. AdSure also reviews your creative, ad text, landing page, tracking and Indian ad rules, for both platforms in one place.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT or another AI?

General chatbots don't see your ad and can make up rules. AdSure's AI looks at your actual ad and answers from checked ad research, and a real ad expert reviews your creative too.

Do I pay anything now?

No. Joining early access is free. You pay ₹1,599 a month only when your beta seat opens and you decide to start.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. The plan is month to month with no lock-in. Cancel anytime and you won't be charged again, and you keep access until the end of the month you've paid for. Plan payments aren't refunded. The details are in our Refund and Cancellation Policy at www.adsure.in/refund-policy.

What happens after 500 people join?

The beta is limited to 500 members so we can give everyone proper attention. After that, new sign-ups go on the waitlist.

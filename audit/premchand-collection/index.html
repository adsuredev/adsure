1HighMETA_LP_001

Ad and page don't match on the books

What we saw: The ad says 6 books including Godan. The page lists 5 books (no Godan) and its link says 8.

Fix: Make the ad, the page title and the book list say the same number and titles.

Discount in the ad is bigger than on the page

What we saw: The ad says 50% off. The page shows ₹999 against ₹1,500, about 33% off.

Fix: Quote the discount the buyer actually gets. A bigger number in the ad reads as bait.
